bench-host: add parent SIGTERM/SIGINT trap to prevent grandchild orphan leaks on pause

bench-host.sh had a per-cell cleanup trap inside run_cell (kills probes
between cells) but NO trap on the bench-host process itself. When the
orchestrator SIGTERMs bench-host (eg the 2026-05-16 pause+commit),
llama-server, bench-cell.py, and the power/thermal probes reparented to
init and continued running. The orchestrator's "stopped" state was a
lie — manual `pkill` was needed on each host to clear the orphans.

Fix: parent-level trap on SIGTERM/SIGINT that wipes the bench-host
process group with -KILL, stops the engine helper, and belt-and-
suspenders kills any straggling probe/bench-cell processes by name.
