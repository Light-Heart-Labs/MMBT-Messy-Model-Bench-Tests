Frequently asked questions

What is a business password manager?

A password manager is a tool that securely stores and manages all the passwords you use to access your different accounts and systems. A business password manager has unique features that go beyond a standard password manager, typically advanced functionalities for team and administrative use and extra account security, to serve businesses’ specific collaborative, organizational, and compliance needs.

How does Proton Pass improve business security?

Proton Pass increases the account security for your entire organization by making it easier for your employees to create and remember strong, unique passwords and use 2FA on all their accounts. It also encrypts your passwords and other sensitive information using end-to-end encryption, keeping them secure even in the event of a data breach.

Additionally, admins have more control with a password manager, including the ability to manage and monitor users, giving them visibility into the/ organization’s security.

How does Proton Pass improve your team’s productivity?

Proton Pass simplifies account security for your team by streaming secure password creation and 2FA implementation. It makes it very simple for your team to follow the standard IT security protocols, allowing them to focus on what they do best. Proton Pass is quick, easy to use, and provides advanced protection. It is the single tool solution for your business’s passwords.

What onboarding help does Proton give to business?

Our Proton Pass for Business support center has guides and advice to help you set up your account. You can also contact our customer support team for assistance.

How does Proton Pass help me be compliant?

All Proton products are designed to help you comply with data regulations, including the GDPR, HIPAA, and NIS2. The data you store with Proton Pass is end-to-end encrypted and cannot be accessed by anyone except you and those you've granted access to. You can find out more about our security model.

How does Proton Pass reduce my security cost?

By helping you prevent data breaches, malware attacks, and loss of your business network, Proton Pass reduces the risk that you'll lose money due to downtime, lost business, or legal fines. Investing in improving your identity and access management will protect your business over the long term. Proton Pass also reduces your operational costs by reducing the number of IT helpdesk support tickets within your business. Operational costs are also reduced by being able to deploy Proton Pass immediately for  your managed and unmanaged applications, reducing downtime during deployment. You also won't be charged additional fees for integrating SSO with your unmanaged applications.

What happens if Proton Pass is breached?

All the data you enter into Proton Pass is end-to-end encrypted before it's stored on our servers. This data can only be accessed using your account password, which we never have access to. Therefore, even in the event of a major data breach at Proton, your encrypted items on Proton Pass (as well as any emails and attachments on Proton Mail, files on Proton Drive, and events on Proton Calendar), would remain securely encrypted and inaccessible to the attacker. Learn more about how our zero-access encryption keeps your data safe.

How do I switch to Proton Pass?

If you're looking to switch to Proton Pass for Business, it's simple. Just sign up for a Proton Pass for Business account, and you can import any existing passwords from your previous password manager.
