    Proton Pass has apps for Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, and Linux. It is also available through web app and browser extensions, including Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox, Brave and more.

    Frequently asked questions

    A password manager helps you create secure passwords and store them safely. It can also autofill password fields and save all your personal information so you can use the internet more efficiently. It's a tool to help you protect yourself online and make life easier.

    When you're choosing a password manager, look for an easy-to-use tool that will sync across your devices. A good password manager should help you create secure passwords, autofill login fields, make it easy to share credentials using secure links, and store more than just passwords (like your credit card details or secure notes).

    Yes! Proton Pass protects your passwords with the same proven end-to-end encryption technology that secures over 100 million Proton accounts. With end-to-end encryption, your data is never accessible to us — only you can decrypt it using your secret password. Proton Pass encrypts all fields (in addition to the password field), including usernames, web addresses, and more.

    Our free and paid plans offer the same level of privacy protection and cover the basic use cases of a password manager (for example, creating, storing, and autofilling unlimited logins on unlimited devices). However, we recommend subscribing to our paid plan if you'd like access to premium features such as:

    • With a paid Proton plan you can create unlimited vaults. Once you reach 50 vaults, just reach out and we can help you create more.
    • Secure sharing of single items with anyone (even if they don't use Proton Pass)
    • Integrated 2FA authenticator
    • Unlimited hide-my-email aliases to protect your actual email address from spam and tracking
    • Security features such as dark web monitoring alerts

    Proton's vision is to build a better internet that's more private and free. That's why we'll always offer a free plan with no ads, catches, or gimmicks. This is only possible thanks to the support of our paying users.
