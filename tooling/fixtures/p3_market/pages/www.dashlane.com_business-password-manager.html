AI-Powered Autofill

Reduce credential mishandling and boost employee productivity by autofilling logins with a local machine learning engine trained for accuracy across web apps.

Passkeys

Enable passwordless access across devices. Dashlane stores passkeys for businesses in secure enclaves instead of on the device, protecting employee credentials even if an endpoint is compromised.

Password Generator

Password Health
