Your security is our priority

At Proton, trust is at the core of everything we do. As a leader in privacy and compliance, we design all our services with your business's security and privacy in mind.

Learn more about Proton Security

The Proton Trust Center is where you’ll find the essential information about our security practices, compliance, and certifications — helping you make the best decision about safeguarding your business and customer data.

End-to-end encryption

At the foundation of the Proton ecosystem is end-to-end encryption (E2EE), keeping your data secure and private at all times.

Data ownership

You retain full control of your data. We cannot access, use, or share it — period.

Protected by Swiss laws

Switzerland’s strong privacy laws ensure your business receives the highest level of legal protection and peace of mind.

Proton security models

All Proton services are built with a secure-by-design approach to protect your data. Our security models define what each product is built to protect you against, ensuring transparency about our capabilities. By always refining our apps and investing in advanced cryptographic architecture, we maintain the highest security standards.

Frequently asked questions

Compliance
What regulations is Proton subject to?

Proton aims to operate in compliance with as many data protection frameworks as possible, to support the maximum number of people around the world. As a Swiss company, this means not only staying up to date with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) but also the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and others.

GDPR
Does Proton have a DPO (data protection officer)?

A data protection officer (DPO) is mandatory under specific circumstances (Art. 37 GDPR) but may also be appointed voluntarily. Proton AG has no designated DPO at this time; however, our legal team is fully equipped to handle data protection requests and can be contacted via email: legal@proton.me.

Security
What certifications does Proton have?

Proton is ISO 27001 certified, which is the international gold standard for information security management. We also hold a SOC 2 Type II attestation, further providing transparency into our security, privacy, and confidentiality controls.

Security
What is a security model?

A security model defines how a product is designed to protect your data and what it is capable of safeguarding against. It explains the protections in place and the limitations, offering transparency about how we keep your information secure.

Open source
Are Proton apps open source?

Yes, all Proton apps are open source. This means that anyone can verify that our apps do exactly what we claim they do. Explore our GitHub repositories(new window) to learn more.

Support
Does Proton offer support for businesses?

Yes, Proton provides 24/7 support for all customers. Business Suite customers also benefit from a dedicated account manager who can assist with everything from onboarding to ongoing needs.

Security
What is Proton's Bug Bounty Program?

Proton encourages security researchers to responsibly disclose vulnerabilities through our Bug Bounty Program. This program rewards researchers for helping us improve security and ensures we address issues proactively.

Industries
What industries can benefit from Proton's services?

Proton’s secure tools are designed for industries like healthcare, nonprofits, finance, legal, and more. Our solutions help our users meet regulatory requirements like GDPR and HIPAA, ensuring privacy and security for all organizations.
