Frequently asked questions

Is Keeper compliant with industry regulations like HIPAA, GDPR and SOC 2?

Yes, Keeper is fully compliant with major industry regulations. Our platform is SOC 2 Type II audited, HIPAA compliant and certified by TrustArc for GDPR and CCPA compliance. Keeper also meets the requirements of ISO 27001, 27017 and 27018, and offers features that support customers in maintaining compliance with various data protection and security standards. For organizations with stricter regulatory needs, additional capabilities like advanced reporting and compliance reports can be added on.

How does Keeper protect data from breaches or insider threats?

Keeper protects data using a zero-knowledge security architecture, which ensures that only the user, not even Keeper, can access vault contents. All data is encrypted locally on the user's device before it ever reaches Keeper's servers. Role-Based Access Controls (RBAC), detailed activity logging and secure sharing features help prevent unauthorized access and limit insider risk. Admins can monitor usage and enforce security policies organization-wide, minimizing exposure to both external threats and internal misuse.

What support does Keeper offer for business users?

Keeper provides 24x7 support to all business users via live chat and email, ensuring help is always available when you need it. Our team assists with everything from onboarding and deployment to troubleshooting and optimization. Business customers also have access to user guides and knowledge base resources to streamline setup and ongoing management.
