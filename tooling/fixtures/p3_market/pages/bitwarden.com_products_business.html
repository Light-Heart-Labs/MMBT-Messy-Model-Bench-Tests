Go passwordless with passkey management

The Bitwarden Password Decisions Survey found that nearly half (49%) of respondents were deploying or had plans to deploy passwordless technology. 

Bitwarden supports passwordless authentication with solutions such as SSO with trusted devices and passkeys.

Bitwarden users can enable passkeys through account settings. Once enabled, the passkey authenticates and decrypts without the need for a master password, making the login process even more seamless.

Frequently Asked Questions

A business password manager is an application that generate, manages, and stores passwords and other secure information in a digital vault with administrative features for businesses. For enterprises, check out the Enterprise Password Manager. Interested in seeing it in action? Reserve a spot at a weekly live demo!

Password managers empower employees to create, manage and store passwords securely. This reduces the risk of the most common cyber threat: weak, reused, or compromised passwords. Bitwarden brings value to your business by reducing risk and increasing productivity!

G2 and Techradar have ranked Bitwarden as the best password manager for small businesses because it is open-source and delivers advanced features to keep users secure.

Cumulus Global report states that 43% of small and medium businesses have been the target of a cyber attack. Hackers view SMB data as an easy mark because they assume smaller businesses don't have the robust security or protection that big corporations can afford.

Many businesses use outdated and unsecure methods for managing passwords like shared spreadsheets - or even written notes. To protect your business from cyber attacks, use end-to-end encrypted password managers to share and store passwords securely.

A small business password manager like Bitwarden Teams focuses on ease of use and affordability. An enterprise password manager like Bitwarden Enterprise adds advanced features like SSO, directory sync, and self-hosting. Both provide complete business password management with admin controls and team sharing.

Start with your IT team to establish policies and test workflows. Use directory sync to automate onboarding. Roll out department by department with training materials. Monitor adoption through reporting, and provide ongoing support for questions.

With Bitwarden zero-knowledge encryption architecture, your vault data remains encrypted and unreadable. Your data is encrypted locally on your device before it ever reaches Bitwarden servers, and only you hold the encryption keys to decrypt it. Not even Bitwarden can access your passwords or sensitive information. Additionally, Bitwarden uses Microsoft Azure managed services to help manage server infrastructure and security, and undergoes third-party security audits to validate its security practices. This means that in the unlikely event of a breach, attackers would only gain access to encrypted data that is computationally infeasible to crack. Your master password never leaves your device, ensuring your vault remains secure regardless of external threats.

Self-hosting gives you complete control over where your password data physically resides, which simplifies compliance with strict data residency and sovereignty requirements like GDPR, data localization laws, or industry-specific regulations. You manage the infrastructure and can deploy Bitwarden on your own servers or private cloud within your geographic jurisdiction. This eliminates concerns about third-party data storage while maintaining the same security standards, certifications, and features as Bitwarden Cloud. Self-hosting is particularly valuable for organizations in regulated industries like healthcare, finance, or government that require data to remain within specific geographic boundaries or under direct organizational control.
