Frequently asked questions

NordPass Free offers all the essential features such as unlimited password storage, automatic sync across devices, autosave, and autofill. You can also set up multi-factor authentication for added security.

The Premium plan provides all the same features as well as password sharing and the ability to switch between devices without the need to log in. It also offers extra security features such as Password Health, which helps to identify weak, old, and reused passwords, and Data Breach Scanner, which looks for your data in any known breaches.

You can easily switch to a different plan in your Nord Account profile. See our Help Center for a guide on upgrading from Free to Premium or Family. Please note that you cannot switch from the Premium to the Family plan directly. If you wish to do so, please contact our Customer Support at [email protected].

The NordPass Family plan allows you to create and use six separate encrypted vaults with access to all Premium features. One of the vaults is for you as the plan’s owner. You can decide who you want to share the remaining five vaults with – whether that’s your friends or family.

If you wish to transfer your Personal account to Business you can purchase the new plan using your current account. Once you’ve bought the plan, you will automatically switch to the NordPass Business account. This switch will not remove any items currently stored in your personal vault. Learn more about converting to the Business plan in our Help Center.
